Our story

Khmer Hearth opened with one wood stove and one idea: cook like grandmother did. We buy vegetables from local farms and grind our spice pastes by hand every morning.

Why guests return

  • Fresh ingredients from Cambodian farms
  • Vegetarian and halal options
  • Free Wi-Fi and a quiet garden patio
